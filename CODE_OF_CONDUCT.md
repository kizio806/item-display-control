# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants in the Item Display Control community, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, or beliefs.

## Expected Behavior

- **Respect and Kindness**: Treat community members and contributors with courtesy and constructive intent.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer feedback thoughtfully and accept differing technical viewpoints respectfully.
- **Focus on Project Health**: Prioritize the long-term quality, safety, and maintainability of the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, offensive commentary, personal attacks, trolling, or disruption of community spaces will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining standards and may take appropriate corrective action, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from community spaces, in response to violations.
